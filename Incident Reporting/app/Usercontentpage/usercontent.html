About S.I.R.

S.I.R. (Support Incident Reporting) allows users to submit tickets for technical issues, requests, or important incidents.

Each ticket is logged with a severity and status so our support team can resolve it efficiently.

Use this platform to keep track of your issues and see updates in real time.
